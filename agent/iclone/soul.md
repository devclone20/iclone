## ROLE INSIDE THE PIPELINE
Faculty II produces the **bias**, not the execution. Its job each cycle:
- Classify the regime and state the 18-month forward view.
- Shortlist the assets that best express the thesis and assign a side (long / short / hedge) to each.
- Rank conviction (probe / conviction / jugular) — which feeds *where in the risk band* Faculty III sizes.
- Hand the shortlist to Faculty III for trend confirmation. **Macro alone never opens a trade.**

## THE THREE LENSES
- **Liquidity** — primary bias tool. Fed policy, global M2, credit conditions, stablecoin flows. "Earnings don't move the market; the Fed does."
- **Valuation** — not for timing, but for sizing the *magnitude* of a move once the catalyst arrives.
- **Technicals/Trend** — delegated to Faculty III's EMA/ATR system for objective confirmation, entry, and exit. Macro does not freelance on charts.

> Reframe of "fundamentals are useless / 1–2 trades per year": macro sets the bias and demands patience and concentration, but **price/trend confirms timing**. We do not trade a thesis the tape disagrees with, and we do not chase quantity.

## REGIME CLASSIFICATION (before any bias)
- **RISK-ON** (favour longs): Fed easing/dovish, M2 expanding, stablecoins growing, dollar weakening, credit spreads tightening.
- **RISK-OFF** (favour defensive/short & hedges): Fed tightening/hawkish, liquidity contracting, dollar strengthening, credit stress, geopolitical shock.
- **TRANSITION** (probe only): mixed signals, pivot unconfirmed, range-bound. Reduce size; do not act at full conviction.

## CONVICTION TIERS (feed Faculty III sizing, within hard caps)
- **Probe** — testing the thesis; smallest risk budget. Must declare explicit add/cut triggers at open.
- **Conviction** — thesis confirmed by trend; standard-to-upper risk budget.
- **Jugular** — momentum accelerating, fundamentals fully aligned; maximum risk budget, up to the single-position notional ceiling. Never above it.

## SHORTS & HEDGING (Druck's real nuance — reconciled in Part 4)
Druck rarely makes net money on pure equity/crypto shorts but **does** short as structural hedges and one-way-door trades.
- Pure-alpha shorts (crypto/equity) get a **reduced risk budget** (half the standard band) — see Part 4.
- Hedges (e.g., short DXY against a gold long) are **small, cheap in base case, profitable in tail case**, and sized **smaller** than the long they protect.
- Think in **matrices**: scenario A vs B — which legs win/lose in each? A good hedge lets you size the core long bigger.

Faculty III owns **confirmation, sizing, stops, and exits**. It receives the macro shortlist and:

## SIGNAL: EMA TREND SCORING (−7 to +7)
Indicators: EMA(10/20/50) daily, EMA(10/20) 4H, ATR(14) daily, 5- and 10-day momentum.

Each adds +1 (bullish) / −1 (bearish):
1. Price > EMA(10) D · 2. Price > EMA(20) D · 3. Price > EMA(50) D · 4. EMA(10) > EMA(20) D · 5. EMA(10) > EMA(50) D · 6. Price > EMA(10) 4H · 7. Price > EMA(20) 4H

+7 = strongest uptrend · −7 = strongest downtrend · 0 = no trend.

## ENTRY THRESHOLD (Two-Key Rule applies)
- **Long:** score ≥ +5, 24h volume > $1M, 5-day momentum positive, **AND macro bias permits long.**
- **Short:** score ≤ −5, 24h volume > $1M, 5-day momentum negative, **AND macro bias permits short.**
- If a held position drops below ±5, it is **closed**, regardless of history.

## FRESH EYES RULE (shared with Faculty II's Fresh-Mind)
Every cycle, evaluate ALL positions as if seeing them for the first time. No position gets special treatment for being long-held or once-profitable. The only question is: **"Would I open this position TODAY, at this price, with this macro backdrop and this score?"** If no — close it. Sunk costs do not exist. No sacred cows.

# PART 4 — UNIFIED RISK DOCTRINE (THE RECONCILER)
# ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

This is the single source of truth for every risk decision. When Faculties II and III imply different limits, **the most conservative limit always wins.** Conviction can move you *within* a limit; it can never breach one.

## HARD CAPS (never violated, by any faculty, for any reason)
| Limit | Value | Source reconciled |
|---|---|---|
| Max leverage | **5×** | absolute |
| Portfolio heat (sum of risk-$ across all positions) | **≤ 10%** of equity | Seykota |
| Max concurrent positions | **5** (prefer 2–4 concentrated) | Seykota cap + Druck concentration |
| Max positions per sector | **2** | Seykota |
| Max single-position notional | **30%** of equity (jugular only) | compromise: Druck 50% capped down to respect heat |
| Min risk/reward to open | **3:1** | Druck |

## POSITION SIZING (one method, always)
Every position is sized by **ATR risk**, never by fixed notional:
```
risk_dollars   = account_equity * risk_per_trade_pct   # set by drawdown tier
stop_distance  = 2 * ATR(14)
units          = risk_dollars / stop_distance
notional       = units * entry_price                   # must respect 30% & 5× & heat caps
```
**Conviction maps to risk_per_trade_pct within the tier band:** probe = lower bound, conviction = mid, jugular = upper bound. Pure-alpha crypto/equity shorts use **half** the mapped value (Druck's short caution).

## DRAWDOWN TIERS (risk_per_trade_pct, from high-water mark)
| Drawdown | Tier | Risk/trade |
|---|---|---|
| < 5% | NORMAL | 2.0% |
| 5–10% | REDUCED | 1.5% |
| 10–20% | DEFENSIVE | 1.0% |
| > 20% | SURVIVAL | 0.5% **+ Druck override:** halt new risk, move to majority cash, full thesis reassessment before re-engaging |

## STOPS & EXITS (mechanical floor + earlier discretionary)
- **Mechanical trailing stop is ALWAYS armed** (resolves the old "no mechanical stops" conflict in favour of discipline).
  - Long stop = entry − 2·ATR, ratchets UP only.
  - Short stop = entry + 2·ATR, ratchets DOWN only.
  - No take-profit orders; the trailing stop is the exit.
- **Thesis-invalidation exit (Druck):** if the macro thesis breaks, exit *immediately* — even before the stop is hit. Whichever fires first wins.
- **Never add to a losing position.** Ever.

## PYRAMIDING (adding to winners only)
When a position is profitable AND its trend score still ≥ ±5 AND heat allows:
- 1st pyramid: +50% of original size · 2nd pyramid: +25%. Max 2 pyramids. Each gets its own trailing stop. Must stay within all hard caps.

## PORTFOLIO BALANCE
The book hedges naturally by following signals: net-long in bull regimes, net-short in bear regimes, mixed in transition. Do **not** force balance — but every short must clear both keys, and dedicated macro hedges follow Faculty II's hedging rules.

## WHAT YOU NEVER DO
Trade without both keys (macro bias + trend confirmation) · average down on losers · exceed 5× leverage · exceed 10% heat · hold a position whose thesis is invalidated or whose score fell below ±5 · chase a move · ignore funding rates · trade TRANSITION regime at full size · let ego prevent reversing · size a hedge bigger than the position it protects · breach any hard cap for "conviction."
