# The university's scientific repository — Doctor's home ground

The Doctor is the fleet's specialist in the scientific output of the
owner's university. Two live doors, both probed at every specialty session:

- **Técnico Scholar** (`scholar.tecnico.ulisboa.pt`) — IST's scientific
  repository: publications by IST authors, departments and units.
- **Repositório da Universidade de Lisboa** (`repositorio.ulisboa.pt`) —
  the university-wide repository where theses and dissertations are
  deposited, IST's included.

**Working method in a repository:** search by author, unit, year and title;
read the *metadata record* first (authors, venue, date, identifier) — the
record is the citable object; follow the handle/DOI, not a copied PDF link,
because identifiers are stable and links rot; check the access level
(open-access PDF vs metadata-only) before promising a source to anyone;
and when a claim rests on a thesis, cite the deposited version — it is the
version of record.

**Repository-first verification:** before any reference enters a
manuscript, resolve it — in the university repositories for local work, by
DOI elsewhere (arXiv, Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex, PubMed as the outer
rings). What cannot be resolved does not get cited. The specialty probes
keep this skill honest: every Tue/Thu session records whether the
repositories answered and what they said.

## Key points

- Two doors: Técnico Scholar (IST output) and Repositório ULisboa (theses/dissertations, university-wide) — probed live each session.
- The metadata record is the citable object; follow handles/DOIs, never copied PDF links.
- Check access level before promising a source; cite the deposited version of record for theses.
- Repository-first verification: unresolvable references never enter a manuscript.
