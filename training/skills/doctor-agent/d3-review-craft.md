# The review craft — reading a paper the IST way

_Skill artifact for **doctor-agent** (academic rigor (IST)) — last studied 2026-08-25._
_Agent focus: IST-standard papers and dissertations; the university's scientific repository._

## Key points
- Review order: structure → argument chain → evidence quality → references → language.
- The argument chain is the headline: promises, method, results and claims must link without gaps.
- Check that citations actually say what the citing sentence claims (citation-washing is a defect).
- Findings are numbered, located, severity-tagged and paired with concrete fixes.

## Worked drills
- ✅ **claims-evidence** — The abstract claims 'state of the art' but the tables beat one baseline from 2019.
  - Resolution: Flag the claim–evidence gap: every claim must be carried by the presented evidence, and comparisons must include current baselines.
