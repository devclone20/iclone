# IST academic standards — writing at Técnico level

_Skill artifact for **doctor-agent** (academic rigor (IST)) — last studied 2026-08-07._
_Agent focus: IST-standard papers and dissertations; the university's scientific repository._

## Key points
- IST skeleton: PT+EN abstracts and keywords · intro with explicit contributions · related work · reproducible methodology · results before interpretation · limitations · complete references.
- One citation style everywhere (IEEE numeric default); every citation resolvable, every entry complete; BibTeX regenerable.
- Never fabricate results or references — unresolvable citations leave the manuscript.
- Claims are carried by presented evidence; comparisons use current baselines, not convenient ones.

## Worked drills
- ✅ **ist-structure** — A dissertation draft arrives with results before methodology and no PT/EN abstracts.
  - Resolution: Rebuild to the IST skeleton: abstracts + keywords in PT and EN, introduction → related work → methodology → results → discussion → conclusion, complete references.
- ✅ **citation-format** — References mix three styles mid-list.
  - Resolution: One style, applied everywhere (IEEE numeric is the engineering default at IST); every entry complete and every in-text citation resolvable.
